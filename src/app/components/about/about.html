About us

Two decades of makeovers

For 20 years, Bride Herbal Beauty parlour has been the neighbourhood's trusted destination for complete transformations. What began as a small herbal-care studio is today known for stunning bridal makeovers — makeup, mehndi, hair, facials, waxing and threading, all under one roof.

Our artists don't just do services — they craft looks. From a bride's big day to your everyday glow-up, every appointment gets the same honest craftsmanship, hygiene and herbal care we have stood for since day one.
